# Privacy Policy for YouTube Momentify

**Last updated: March 6, 2026**

YouTube Momentify helps users save and organize timestamp bookmarks for YouTube videos.

## Data Storage

All bookmark data, notes, tags, and user preferences are stored locally in the user’s browser. This information remains on the user’s device and is not transmitted to external servers.

## Data Collection

The extension may access information from YouTube pages such as video titles, video IDs, and timestamps in order to create and manage bookmarks. This data is used only for the functionality of the extension.

## Data Sharing

YouTube Momentify does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data with third parties.

## User Control

Users can delete their bookmarks and preferences at any time by removing the extension or clearing the extension’s storage.

## Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
